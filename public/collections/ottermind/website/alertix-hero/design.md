## 概述

Alertix was captured at 1932x1400 and kept as a specification rather than as a gallery image. The whole page is built on deep blue #0A0F14 underneath, muted teal #C3DFE8 for the type and blue #1D1D6D carrying the accents. It is the right reference for an AI or SaaS product hero section that has to look deliberate on the first screen.

## 布局

**The nav is deliberately thin** — Links sit on one line at a single size, and the #1D1D6D action closes the row on the right so the eye finishes where the navigation ends. The links are set below the wordmark in size, and the #1D1D6D action is the only element in the bar with any weight.

**Values you can check** — The layout is solved against fixed values (120px spacing steps), which is what keeps the first screen composed at any width. The list is short enough that a reviewer can check it element by element.

**Video at the bottom layer** — The first screen is a single clip at 1932x1400, and everything else is arranged on top of it. A poster frame at 1932x1400 holds the composition when autoplay is unavailable, so the layout never collapses. Export at 1932x1400 or wider to keep the crop honest.

**Links before the action** — The bar sits over the 1932x1400 composition without a background of its own, so the media runs edge to edge underneath it.


## 动效

**The loop does the ambient work** — Ambient movement comes from the 7.9s 1932x1400 loop rather than from scroll effects, which keeps the page calm and the type readable. Because it is muted, the loop can run under the copy without competing for sound or focus.

**Hover where it means something** — Only the controls respond to the pointer (hover states on the buttons and links rather than on every card), so the page feels responsive without turning every element into a target. Feedback is limited to the controls, so the composition stays still around them.


## 可替换项

- Hold the hero section to one screen on desktop; the brief never asks the reader to scroll past the fold.
- Swap the clip for your own footage at 1932x1400 or wider, keep it muted, and match the 7.9s loop point.
- Replace the headline and the supporting line with your own copy, keeping the same line count so the entrance still lands over the 1932x1400 frame.
- Change the copy first, then the accent, then the media: the 1932x1400 frame was solved in that order and reversing it costs a re-layout.

## 文案规则

**What to change first in Alertix** — A template asks you to accept its layout; this reference asks you to keep it and change everything inside. The difference is that 120px spacing steps stay fixed while the subject moves. The subject is the part that should change.

**How the hero section reflows** — Responsive here means the scale changes and the sequence does not. the navigation collapses into a mobile menu, and the reading order stays where the brief put it. The order is the last thing to change.

**How Alertix sequences the first read** — When a SaaS reader lands on a single screen, they scan before they read. The layout answers that: navigation and the background clip are sized to be scanned, with 120px spacing steps keeping each stop distinct. A SaaS reader gets the offer before the options.
