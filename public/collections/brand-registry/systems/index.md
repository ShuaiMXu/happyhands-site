# Systems

- [Meridian Mono](http://localhost:8080/systems/meridian-mono): Sharp and mono, for infrastructure and data tools
- [Meridian Glass](http://localhost:8080/systems/meridian-glass): Glassy and compact, for AI products
- [Meridian Slate](http://localhost:8080/systems/meridian-slate): Cold and calm, for AI agents and developer tools
- [Meridian Clay](http://localhost:8080/systems/meridian-clay): Warm and light, for consumer apps

Each one installs from a shadcn registry.
