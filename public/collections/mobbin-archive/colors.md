## Frequently asked questions

### What is a color palette and how do you create one?

A color palette is a set of colors used together in a design system. Most palettes include primary, secondary, and neutral tones that work harmoniously. You can create one by using color wheel principles—like complementary or analogous relationships—or by testing combinations for contrast and usability.

### What is color psychology and why is it used in design?

Color psychology looks at how different hues influence emotions, perception, and behavior. In design, it's used to guide user experience—like building trust with blue, sparking attention with red, or signaling success with green.

### Where can I explore brand colors used by real companies?

### What are color codes and how do they work across design formats?

Color codes define how colors appear across digital and print systems. Formats like HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK each represent colors differently depending on context. You can explore color codes and meanings to see how each format is used and how they convert between systems.
