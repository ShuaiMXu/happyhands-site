## 布局

**Links before the action** — Navigation is a thin strip rather than a band: wordmark left, links centre-right, one #0B2A3A action, all of it sitting above the landing page without a background of its own. Below 360px the links collapse instead of wrapping onto a second line.

**Media behind the type** — The background is a single 6.9s loop at 1920x1422, cropped to cover and left un-tinted. Everything else floats above it on a higher layer. A poster frame at 1920x1422 holds the composition when autoplay is unavailable, so the layout never collapses.

**A list, not a card wall** — The features are set as a list in Plus Jakarta Sans rather than as a wall of cards. The list is left-aligned to the same margin as the headline.

**Top row, one action** — Design the collapsed state before adding a sixth link. The reference was captured at 1920x1422.


## 动效

**Pointer states, nothing more** — The page answers only where it can be acted on: hover states on the buttons and links rather than on every card. Each state changes one property, which keeps the feedback legible. Check the states on a touch device.

**Motion can be switched off** — Reduced motion is specified rather than bolted on: with it on, the entrance and the loop stops and the composition holds its final state. The layout is identical with the motion disabled, so nothing depends on the animation.

## 文案规则

**Reading TrueEarth as a spec** — Type is the first number: type from 12px to 16px and 100vh sections. The spacing follows from it, and everything else on the page is positioned against those two values. Nothing in this landing page depends on a default.

**What moves in TrueEarth** — Motion is limited to 72ms transitions, a 6.9s media loop and a masked line reveal. Nothing in the landing page animates for longer than the brief allows. Nothing animates for its own sake in this landing page.

**How TrueEarth sequences the first read** — One screen, one action, and navigation, the headline, the announcement badge, the stats row and the card grid between them.
