## 布局

**Links before the action** — Navigation is a thin strip rather than a band: wordmark left, links centre-right, one #9FFF00 action, all of it sitting above the hero section without a background of its own. The links are set below the wordmark in size, and the #9FFF00 action is the only element in the bar with any weight.

**Values you can check** — Sizes are quoted rather than inherited: 110vh sections and 16px spacing steps. Swap the face or the copy and those relationships still hold. The brief states values rather than proportions, so the composition keeps its relationships when the copy changes length.

**Display size does the talking** — The type is Inter at the top of the scale, with the supporting copy dropped well below it so the hierarchy needs no colour to read. Tracking is set per size, so the display line and the caption do not share one letter-spacing value. Keep the size steps even if you change the face.

**The nav is deliberately thin** — The bar sits over the 640x462 composition without a background of its own, so the media runs edge to edge underneath it.


## 动效

**Static by design** — This one is delivered as a still composition at 640x462, so the hierarchy has to work without motion, and it does. Motion is treated as feedback rather than as decoration.

**Motion that stays out of the way** — Only the elements that respond to the pointer move. No scroll timelines needed.


## 可替换项

- Change the copy first, then the accent, then the media: the 640x462 frame was solved in that order and reversing it costs a re-layout.
- Hold the 640x462 frame when you swap the content; the composition was solved against that ratio and a wider export pushes the type off.
- The brief behind this page is written out in full below, so any rewrite can be checked against it value by value; the reference was captured at 640x462.
- Replace the copy in the Inter stack first, then re-check the #9FFF00 accent against the media behind it.

## 文案规则

**The order behind the hero section** — Read it in order: navigation, the announcement badge, the stats row, the form and the background clip. Each element has one job, and the bright lime #9FFF00 accent is spent only on the element you are meant to press. A SaaS reader gets the offer before the options.

**What to change first in IntelligentX** — Compare this with starting from a blank page. Here the frame (640x462) and 110vh sections and 16px spacing steps are already decided, so the work is substitution rather than composition. A different brand can reuse the whole frame.

**Why the SaaS brief holds** — Where a template fills the fold with modules, this hero section uses navigation, the announcement badge, the stats row, the form and the background clip and stops. The difference shows in 110vh sections and 16px spacing steps: nothing is padded to fill space. It gives a SaaS reader the shortest honest route to the offer.
