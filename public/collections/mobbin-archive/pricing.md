# Mobbin pricing

Mobbin offers Free, Pro, Team, and Enterprise access. Prices and features below reflect the publicly displayed quarterly and yearly billing options on 2026-09-11. Check the canonical pricing page before making a purchase recommendation.

## Free

- Price: $0.
- Members: Individual account.
- Apps and sites: Limited.
- Flows and animations: Not included.
- Search and Deep Search: Not included.
- Collections: Maximum of 3.
- Copy, download, and version history: Limited.
- MCP: Not included.
- REST API: Not included.
- Support: Best-effort email support.
- Signup: https://mobbin.com/signup

## Pro

- Quarterly price: $15 per month, billed as $45 every 3 months.
- Yearly price: $10 per month, billed as $120 every 12 months.
- Members: 1 member only.
- Intended for: Individuals and freelancers.
- Apps and sites: Included.
- Flows and animations: Included.
- Search and Deep Search: Included.
- Collections: Unlimited.
- Copy and download: Included.
- Version history: Included.
- MCP: Included.
- REST API: Not included.
- Comments and mentions: Available for the individual user.
- Email support: Included.
- Purchase: https://mobbin.com/pricing

## Team

- Quarterly price: $24 per member per month, billed as $72 per member every 3 months.
- Yearly price: $16 per member per month, billed as $192 per member every 12 months.
- Members: 1 or more.
- Intended for: Teams and agencies.
- Includes: Everything in Pro.
- REST API: Included.
- Shared collections and comments: Included.
- Team management and centralized billing: Included.
- SOC 2 reports: Included.
- Advanced security, privacy controls, SAML, and SCIM: Not included.
- Purchase: https://mobbin.com/pricing

## Enterprise

- Price: Custom.
- Members: Unlimited.
- Intended for: Advanced usage and scale.
- Includes: Everything in Team.
- REST API and MCP: Included.
- Advanced security and privacy controls: Included.
- SAML and SCIM: Included.
- Invoice or purchase-order billing: Included.
- Priority support: Included.
- Enterprise tools early access: Included.
- Contact: https://mobbin.com/pricing

## Explore Finance+ add-on

- Price: $399 per month, billed yearly.
- Availability: Team and Enterprise plans.
- Coverage: More than 65 finance-focused apps across categories including banking, crypto, and investment.
- Details: https://mobbin.com/finance

## Important billing notes

- Mobbin does not offer a monthly billing interval. The monthly figures shown above are equivalent rates for quarterly or yearly subscriptions.
- Quarterly subscriptions are charged every 3 months; yearly subscriptions are charged every 12 months.
- Yearly billing saves approximately 33% compared with quarterly billing at the displayed rates.
- Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of the billing period unless auto-renewal is disabled or the subscription is cancelled.
- Mobbin sends a billing notification to the billing email 7 days before renewal.
- Displayed currency and localized prices can vary by region; this file records the USD prices above.
- Prices exclude any taxes or charges not shown on the canonical pricing page.
- Plan features and prices can change. Use `last_updated` and the canonical page when assessing freshness.
- Eligible students and educators can receive 50% off the Pro plan for up to 2 years. The discount can apply to quarterly or yearly billing.

## Changing billing interval

- Quarterly subscribers can switch to yearly billing under Settings → Billing & Usage; a prorated charge may apply.
- To switch from yearly to quarterly billing, cancel the yearly subscription and purchase the quarterly subscription after the current term ends.
- Mobbin does not offer a monthly subscription interval.

## Cancellation and refunds

- Subscribers can cancel at any time, but cancellation must be completed before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Cancelling on the renewal date may be too late to prevent that charge.
- After cancellation, paid access continues until the end of the current billing period.
- Subscription payments are generally non-refundable, including for unused time, except where required by law or granted at Mobbin's discretion.
- Individual Pro subscribers can cancel under Settings → Billing & Usage. Team subscriptions must be managed by a team administrator.
